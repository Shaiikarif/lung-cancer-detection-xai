01 / OVERVIEW

Research with transparency at its core.

PulmoAI combines a ResNet-50 convolutional backbone with a custom Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM), class-imbalance-aware training, visual explanations, uncertainty estimation, and robustness experiments.

🧠 Attention-guided learning

ResNet-50 features are enhanced with channel and spatial attention through CBAM.

🔎 Multiple explanations

Grad-CAM, Grad-CAM++, LIME, and Integrated Gradients provide complementary attribution views.

📈 Uncertainty awareness

Monte Carlo Dropout estimates predictive variation across repeated stochastic inference passes.

03 / EXPLAINABILITY & ROBUSTNESS

More than a predicted label.

🔥 Grad-CAM

Gradient-based localization heatmaps from final convolutional feature maps.

🎯 Grad-CAM++

An enhanced class activation mapping approach for localization.

🧩 LIME

Superpixel-based local explanations showing regions influencing a prediction.

🧭 Integrated Gradients

Attribution maps computed along a path from baseline to input image.

📊 MC Dropout

Repeated stochastic forward passes estimate predictive mean and variance.

🛡️ Robustness tests

FGSM perturbations and image corruption experiments such as noise and blur.
