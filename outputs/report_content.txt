TITLE:
    Explainable & Robust AI Framework for Early Lung Cancer Detection

--------------------------------------------------------------------

ABSTRACT (Copy this to your report):

    Lung cancer is the leading cause of cancer-related deaths worldwide,
    and early detection significantly improves survival rates. This project
    presents an Explainable and Robust AI Framework for early lung cancer
    detection from CT scan images. The proposed system uses a ResNet50
    backbone enhanced with a Channel and Spatial Attention mechanism
    (CBAM) to classify CT scans into Normal, Benign, and Malignant
    categories. To ensure clinical trustworthiness, the framework integrates
    multiple explainability techniques including Gradient-weighted Class
    Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM), Local Interpretable Model-agnostic
    Explanations (LIME), and Integrated Gradients, providing visual
    explanations that highlight the regions influencing the model's
    decision. Robustness is evaluated through adversarial attack testing
    (FGSM), input corruption analysis, and Monte Carlo Dropout-based
    uncertainty quantification. The system achieves an accuracy of
    98.96%, weighted F1-score of 0.9895, and ROC-AUC of
    0.9999 on the IQ-OTH/NCCD lung cancer dataset. A web-based
    interface built with Flask allows real-time prediction with visual
    explanations, making the system suitable for clinical decision support.
