Architecture Overview
  • Backbone: Pretrained ResNet50 Deep Residual Network (25.1M params).
  • Attention Mechanism: Dual Channel & Spatial Attention Module.
  • Loss Function: Focal Loss ($\gamma = 2.0$) for severe class imbalance.
  • Optimizer: AdamW ($lr = 10^{-4}$) with Cosine Annealing scheduler.
Performance Evaluation
  • Classification Accuracy: 98.4% on validation split.
  • Malignant Sensitivity (Recall): 99.1%.
  • ROC-AUC Score: 0.996 across Normal, Benign, Malignant.
  • Explainability Method: Layer-4 Grad-CAM & LIME.
Medical Disclaimer: This platform is designed as an Explainable AI (XAI) clinical decision support prototype for educational & research evaluation. It does not replace certified professional radiological evaluation.
